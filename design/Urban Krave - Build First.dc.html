S6 · Our story

No dining room. All flavor.

Urban Krave started with a simple complaint: great Mediterranean food in Seattle was either a sit-down dinner or a soggy delivery. So we built a kitchen that only does delivery, and designed every dish to travel. Wraps are pressed so they hold together. Sauces ride in their own cups. Falafel is fried the minute your order lands.

The recipes come from family tables in Beirut, Amman and Istanbul, adjusted for a city that likes its greens and its heat. Chicken and beef shawarma turn on the spit all day, toum is whipped every morning, and the baklava is made in small batches with honey from a farm near Woodinville.

Made to travel

Packaging and recipes tested for a 30-minute ride.

Halal meats

All chicken and beef are halal-certified.

Plant-forward

Every dish has a vegan falafel option.
